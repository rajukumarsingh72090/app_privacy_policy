Privacy Policy for Edumania

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

Welcome to Edumania ("the Application"). We are dedicated to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data transparently and securely. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, processed, and safeguarded when you use the Edumania mobile application and its associated website, developed by Raju Kumar Singh ("Service Provider", "we", "our", or "us").

1. Information Collection and Use

To provide an engaging, high-performance learning experience, the Application and associated website collect and use the following categories of information:

  • Account & Profile Information: When you register or sign in, we collect your name, email address, and profile details. The mobile application and website use the same Edumania account. Passwords are not stored in plain text; they are stored using one-way salted password hashing (bcrypt) in our MongoDB Atlas database. Authentication is managed using JSON Web Tokens (JWT).
  • Website Checkout & Payment Information: When you purchase digital courses through our website, checkout is processed using Razorpay. Razorpay handles payment credentials such as card, UPI, or banking details through its payment flow. Our systems may receive and store transaction references, order IDs, payment status, amount, and purchase details needed to verify the payment and grant access to the purchased course. We do not intentionally store full card numbers, card security codes, UPI PINs, or online banking passwords on Edumania servers. Please review the Razorpay Privacy Notice.
  • Course Progress & Learning Analytics: We store your enrolled courses, lesson completion status, and video playback timestamps to enable seamless learning across devices and automatic lesson resume.
  • Device & Diagnostic Data: The Application may collect technical metadata such as your device model, operating system version, app version, and crash logs to maintain stability and diagnose errors.
  • Website & Hosting Data: Our website is hosted on Vercel. Website hosting and delivery may involve technical data such as IP address, browser and device information, request logs, and security diagnostics as described in Vercel's Privacy Notice.
  • Over-The-Air (OTA) Updates: The Application uses Expo EAS Update to deliver app updates. During update checks, runtime metadata such as app version and platform may be transmitted to determine whether a newer bundle is available.

The Application does not request or track your precise GPS location.

3. Account Deletion and User Rights

You may request deletion of your Edumania account and associated personal data. Because the mobile application and website use the same Edumania account, an account deletion request applies to that shared account and its associated learning records, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

  • In-App Deletion: If available in your app version, navigate to Profile > Settings > Delete Account and follow the instructions. This request is intended to delete your account profile, authentication-related records, and learning progress from our active systems, subject to applicable retention requirements.
  • Email Request: You can also request deletion by emailing rajukumarsingh72090@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request". Please use the email address associated with your Edumania account so we can verify the request.

Order, payment, invoice, fraud-prevention, or accounting records may be retained for the period required by applicable law or legitimate recordkeeping obligations, even if your account is deleted. Such retained records will be restricted to the relevant purposes. For complete details, please review our Account Deletion Policy.

4. Data Security and Encryption

We use technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. These include:

  • HTTPS/TLS for network communications between the mobile application or website and our backend, where configured.
  • JSON Web Tokens (JWT) with expiration controls for authentication.
  • Salted password hashing using bcrypt before passwords are stored.
  • Time-limited verification codes for email verification and password resets, where these features are enabled.
  • Server-side payment verification using Razorpay's API and payment webhooks. Course access should be granted only after the backend validates the payment status and relevant order details.

No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. The specific safeguards depend on the configuration of our application, backend, hosting, and third-party services.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide Edumania services, maintain your account, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and comply with legal or accounting obligations. When an account is deleted, we delete or de-identify associated profile and learning data from active systems where applicable. Limited order, payment-reference, invoice, and transaction records may be retained for legally required or legitimate accounting, security, and dispute-resolution purposes. Backup copies may persist for a limited period before being overwritten under our backup procedures.

6. Children's Privacy

The Application is intended for a general audience and is not knowingly designed to collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without appropriate authorization where required. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us at the email below so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
