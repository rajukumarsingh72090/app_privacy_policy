Account Deletion Policy

Effective Date: September 21, 2026

At Edumania, we believe in user autonomy, privacy, and full data control. In accordance with applicable privacy laws and Google Play Developer Policies, this policy details how you can request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal records.

1. How to Delete Your Account

You have the absolute right to delete your Edumania account at any time. We provide two easy, accessible methods:

Method 1: In-App Deletion (Immediate & Automated)

The fastest way to delete your account is directly inside the Edumania mobile application:

  1. Open the Edumania app on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to your Profile screen (bottom tab or side menu).
  3. Tap on Settings or scroll to the Account Settings section.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Review the confirmation dialog and tap Confirm Delete.

Once confirmed, the application sends a secure API request to our server, which immediately and permanently purges your account from our MongoDB Atlas database and revokes your authentication session.

Method 2: Web / Email Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you no longer have the app installed or cannot access your device, you can submit a manual deletion request:

  1. Send an email from your registered email address to: rajukumarsingh72090@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request - Edumania.
  3. State clearly in the message body that you wish to permanently delete your Edumania account and all associated data.

Our support team will process your request and permanently delete your records within 48 to 72 hours. You will receive an email confirmation once completed.

2. What Personal Data is Permanently Deleted

When your account deletion is processed, the following information is permanently purged from our active systems:

  • Identity & Profile Data: Your name, email address, profile photo, and account creation timestamps.
  • Security Credentials: Encrypted password hashes (bcrypt), one-time verification tokens, and active JWT session keys.
  • Course & Learning Records: All enrolled courses, lesson progress, video timestamps, and curriculum completion history stored in MongoDB Atlas.
  • Device Tokens & Preferences: App preferences, theme configurations, and push notification tokens.

Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered, and you will immediately forfeit access to any previously enrolled course materials.

3. Data Retention Exceptions

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and applicable legal requirements, we may securely retain limited records under the following strict conditions:

  • Financial & Transaction Compliance: Non-identifying Google Play order IDs and purchase verification tokens may be retained for statutory tax, legal accounting, and dispute reconciliation purposes as required by law.
  • Anonymized Security Logs: Temporary, fully anonymized server traffic logs may be kept for a maximum of 30 days solely for fraud prevention and system security before automatic deletion.

No personal user profile, email, or passwords are kept as part of these compliance records.
