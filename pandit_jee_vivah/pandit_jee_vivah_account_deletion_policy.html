🗑️ Google Play Store User Data Compliance Notice

In accordance with Google Play's Data Safety and Account Deletion policies, Pandit Jee Vivah provides a transparent, easy-to-use pathway for all users to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal matrimonial data — both within the Android app and via direct email/web request for users who have uninstalled the app.

1. User's Right to Data Erasure

Every registered user of Pandit Jee Vivah possesses the full right to delete their matrimonial profile and permanently wipe all identifying information, communications, photos, and astrological records at any time. Account deletion is irreversible.

2. How to Delete Your Account

We provide two convenient methods for requesting account and data deletion:

Method 1: Direct In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the Pandit Jee Vivah app installed on your Android device, you can delete your account instantly using our self-service flow:

1 Launch the Pandit Jee Vivah app on your Android device.

2 Navigate to the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.

3 Tap on the Settings (Gear Icon) or "Account & Privacy Settings".

4 Scroll down to the Danger Zone and select "Delete Account & Wipe Data".

5 Read the confirmation summary detailing the permanent erasure of all matches, messages, and photos.

6 Confirm your decision by entering your account password or verifying an OTP sent to your registered email via Brevo.

7 Tap "Permanently Delete My Account". Your account is immediately purged from our servers, your session token is revoked, and you are logged out.

4. Deletion Processing Timeline

  • In-App Deletion: Immediate (< 1 second). Your profile disappears instantly from candidate searches, and all records across database collections are purged in parallel.

5. Data Retention Exceptions & Legal Compliance

What is NOT retained: We do NOT retain any personal profile information, photos, astrological Kundlis, or private chat messages once an account is deleted.

Statutory Financial Records: In strict compliance with Indian financial accounting, Goods and Services Tax (GST) regulations, and anti-fraud standards, financial transaction records pertaining to paid membership upgrades (such as Razorpay transaction reference numbers, Google Play purchase receipt tokens, invoice numbers, amounts, and dates) are retained securely for the statutory record-keeping period required by law. These records are isolated, encrypted, and never used for marketing or re-identification purposes.

6. Confirmation of Account Deletion

  • Email Requests: A final confirmation notice will be dispatched via Brevo to your registered email address confirming complete data erasure.
